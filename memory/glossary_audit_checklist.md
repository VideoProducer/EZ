## TIER 1 — HIGHEST RISK (statutory dollar amounts, tax rates, thresholds)
*These change with each BC budget or federal ruling. Numbers must match current gov.bc.ca guidance.*

### ☐ 1. Property Transfer Tax (PTT)
- Slug: `/glossary/property-transfer-tax-ptt`
- **Facts to verify:**
  - Rate: 1% on first $200K, 2% from $200K–$3M, 3% above $3M, +2% additional on residential portion above $3M
  - Foreign buyer additional PTT rate: 20% (BC-wide since 2018)
  - Statutory reference (PTT Act, RSBC 1996, c. 378)
- **Primary source:** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Statute:** https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96378_01

### ☐ 2. First-Time Home Buyers' Program (FTB PTT Exemption)
- Slug: `/glossary/first-time-home-buyers-program`
- **Facts to verify:**
  - Full exemption threshold (recently raised — verify current cap: as of 2024 update it's $835,000 fair market value with full exemption up to $500K portion)
  - Partial exemption sliding scale threshold
  - Residency requirements (12 months in BC)
- **Primary source:** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers

### ☐ 3. Newly Built Home Exemption
- Slug: `/glossary/newly-built-home-exemption`
- **Facts to verify:**
  - Full exemption fair-market-value cap (as of 2024: $1,100,000)
  - Partial exemption band ceiling (as of 2024: $1,150,000)
  - "Newly built" definition — must be first occupant
- **Primary source:** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption

### ☐ 4. Speculation and Vacancy Tax (SVT)
- Slug: `/glossary/speculation-and-vacancy-tax-svt`
- **Facts to verify:**
  - Rate for Canadian citizens/PR: 0.5%
  - Rate for foreign owners and satellite families: 2%
  - Coverage areas (Metro Van, Fraser Valley, CRD, Nanaimo, Kelowna, West Kelowna, Squamish, Duncan, Ladysmith, Lake Cowichan, plus 2024 expansions to Vernon, Coldstream, Penticton, etc.)
  - Annual declaration deadline (typically March 31)
- **Primary source:** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/speculation-and-vacancy-tax

### ☐ 5. Underused Housing Tax (UHT) — Federal
- Slug: `/glossary/underused-housing-tax-uht`
- **Facts to verify:**
  - Rate: 1% federal (applies to non-resident, non-Canadian owners of vacant/underused residential property)
  - Return deadline (April 30)
  - Recent 2023 exemption expansions (Canadian citizens/PR no longer need to file unless owning through corporation/partnership/trust)
- **Primary source:** https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/underused-housing-tax.html

### ☐ 6. Home Buyer Rescission Period (HBRP)
- Slug: `/glossary/home-buyer-rescission-period-hbrp` (or similar)
- **Facts to verify:**
  - Window: 3 business days after offer acceptance
  - Rescission fee: 0.25% of purchase price (i.e., $250 per $100,000)
  - Property classes covered (detached, semi-detached, townhouse, apartment, residential strata lots, cooperative interests, MHRs) — NOT commercial, NOT vacant land, NOT auction sales, NOT court-ordered sales, NOT prescribed leased land
  - In effect since January 3, 2023
- **Primary source:** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/real-estate-in-bc/home-buyer-rescission-period

### ☐ 7. Home Owner Grant
- Slug: `/glossary/home-owner-grant`
- **Facts to verify:**
  - Basic grant amount ($570 outside Northern/Rural; $770 Northern/Rural)
  - Additional senior/disabled grant ($275 top-up)
  - Assessed value threshold and phase-out (as of 2024: full grant to $2,150,000; phased out $5 per $1,000 above)
  - Who applies (owner-occupier as of December 31 tax year)
- **Primary source:** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant

### ☐ 8. Foreign Buyer Ban (Non-Canadians Act)
- Slug: `/glossary/foreign-buyer-ban` (or similar)
- **Facts to verify:**
  - Federal ban currently extended until January 1, 2027
  - Exemptions (work permit holders with 3+ years remaining, students, refugees, spouses of Canadians)
  - Property types covered (residential, includes 3-unit-or-fewer)
  - Penalty for violation ($10,000 fine + court-ordered sale)
- **Primary source:** https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/P-25.2/
- **Guidance:** https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/industry-innovation-and-leadership/industry-expertise/foreign-buyer-ban

### ☐ 9. BC Home Flipping Tax (2025)
- Slug: `/glossary/anti-flipping-tax` or `/glossary/home-flipping-tax`
- **Facts to verify:**
  - Provincial tax effective January 1, 2025
  - Rate: 20% for property sold within 365 days of purchase, sliding to 0% at 730 days
  - Exemptions (primary residence exemption, life events)
- **Primary source:** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/income-taxes/personal/bc-home-flipping-tax

### ☐ 10. Federal Anti-Flipping Rule (Income Tax Act, since 2023)
- Slug: could be within `/glossary/anti-flipping-tax` combined
- **Facts to verify:**
  - Property sold within 365 days = 100% business income (not capital gain)
  - Exceptions (death, disability, separation, new job >40 km, insolvency)
- **Primary source:** https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/i-3.3/

## TIER 2 — HIGH RISK (statutory duties, timelines, section numbers)

### ☐ 11. Disclosure of Representation in Trading Services (DoRTS)
- Slug: `/glossary/disclosure-of-representation-in-trading-services-dorts`
- **Facts to verify:**
  - When must be provided ("at the earliest opportunity" and always before providing trading services)
  - Content requirements per BCFSA Rules
- **Primary source:** https://www.bcfsa.ca/industry-resources/real-estate-professional-resources/knowledge-base/forms-and-templates
- **Rules:** https://www.bcfsa.ca/industry-resources/real-estate-professional-resources/knowledge-base/legislation-and-rules

### ☐ 12. Disclosure of Interest in Trade (DoIT)
- Slug: likely present as separate term
- **Facts to verify:**
  - When licensee must disclose personal interest
  - Timing before offer
- **Primary source:** BCFSA Rules

### ☐ 13. Working With a REALTOR® Form
- Slug: `/glossary/working-with-a-realtor` (or similar)
- **Facts to verify:**
  - Nature of form (consumer education, replaced older Working With a REALTOR® form under new BCFSA Rules)
  - When it must be provided
- **Primary source:** BCFSA/BC REA/CREA

### ☐ 14. Assignment of Contract (Presale)
- Slug: `/glossary/assignment-of-contract`
- **Facts to verify:**
  - REDMA disclosure requirements for developers (since 2018)
  - Assignment tax reporting to CRA (assignment income treated as business income under 2022 rules)
  - GST treatment (assignor may be liable for GST on assignment fee)
- **Primary source:** https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04041_01

### ☐ 15. Subject Clauses
- Slug: `/glossary/subject-clauses`
- **Facts to verify:**
  - Business days vs. calendar days convention in BC (business days is standard)
  - Subject removal must be in writing
  - Buyer's deposit at risk if subjects removed and buyer doesn't close
- **Primary source:** RESA + Standard BC Real Estate contract

### ☐ 16. Deposit / Trust Money Rules
- Slug: search for "deposit"
- **Facts to verify:**
  - Deposit typically 5% of purchase price (custom, not statute)
  - Must be held in brokerage trust account
  - Release only per contract or court order
- **Primary source:** RESA + BCFSA Rules on trust accounts

### ☐ 17. Multiple Representation (Dual Agency)
- Slug: search for "dual agency" or "multiple representation"
- **Facts to verify:**
  - Prohibited in most residential real estate under BCFSA Rules since 2018 (with narrow exceptions)
  - Exceptions: remote locations without other licensees available
- **Primary source:** BCFSA — Multiple Representation Rules

### ☐ 18. Strata Depreciation Report
- Slug: `/glossary/depreciation-report`
- **Facts to verify:**
  - Requirement: every 5 years (Strata Property Regulation)
  - Applies to strata corps with 5+ units
  - Prepared by qualified person
  - Recent 2024 amendment removed the 3/4 vote to waive
- **Primary source:** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Statute:** https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/43_2000

### ☐ 19. Strata Contingency Reserve Fund (CRF)
- Slug: search "contingency reserve"
- **Facts to verify:**
  - Minimum CRF contribution: 10% of annual operating budget until CRF reaches 25% of operating budget minimum
  - Governed by Strata Property Regulation
- **Primary source:** https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/43_2000

### ☐ 20. Form B Information Certificate
- Slug: search "form b"
- **Facts to verify:**
  - Required contents (bylaws, rules, financial statements, minutes, insurance, current owner accounts)
  - Valid for 60 days
  - Fee cap (currently $35 + strata may charge for photocopies)
- **Primary source:** Strata Property Regulation

### ☐ 21. Form F Certificate of Payment
- Slug: search "form f"
- **Facts to verify:**
  - Confirms current owner has no outstanding strata debt
  - Required at closing
- **Primary source:** Strata Property Regulation

### ☐ 22. Strata Rental Restrictions (Post-2022 Reform)
- Slug: search "rental restriction"
- **Facts to verify:**
  - BC eliminated most strata rental restriction bylaws effective November 24, 2022
  - Retained: 18+ age restriction (originally 55+ before 2022), family occupancy protection
  - Short-term rental (Airbnb) restrictions may still apply per municipal bylaws
- **Primary source:** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing

### ☐ 23. Age-Restricted Strata (55+ vs 19+)
- Slug: search "age restriction"
- **Facts to verify:**
  - 55+ age restriction bylaws grandfathered as of November 24, 2022; no NEW 55+ bylaws allowed
  - Only 55+ bylaws can restrict; other age-restrictions eliminated
- **Primary source:** Strata Property Act amendments

### ☐ 24. Short-Term Rental Accommodations Act (2024)
- Slug: search "short-term rental"
- **Facts to verify:**
  - Principal residence requirement in ~60 designated communities (effective May 1, 2024)
  - Registration and platform accountability requirements
- **Primary source:** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/short-term-rentals

### ☐ 25. Mortgage Stress Test (OSFI B-20)
- Slug: `/glossary/mortgage-stress-test`
- **Facts to verify:**
  - Qualifying rate: contract rate + 2%, OR 5.25% floor, whichever HIGHER
  - Applies to federally-regulated lenders (banks, trust cos)
  - Does not apply to credit unions provincially regulated (BC — but many voluntarily apply)
- **Primary source:** https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/guidance/guidance-library/residential-mortgage-underwriting-practices-procedures-guideline-b-20

### ☐ 26. Gross Debt Service Ratio (GDS)
- Slug: `/glossary/gross-debt-service-ratio`
- **Facts to verify:**
  - Insured mortgage: max 39%
  - Uninsured: max ~35% (lender-dependent)
  - Components: mortgage payment + property tax + heat + 50% strata fees
- **Primary source:** CMHC + OSFI B-20

### ☐ 27. Total Debt Service Ratio (TDS)
- Slug: `/glossary/total-debt-service-ratio`
- **Facts to verify:**
  - Insured mortgage: max 44%
  - Uninsured: max ~42% (lender-dependent)
- **Primary source:** CMHC + OSFI B-20

### ☐ 28. CMHC Mortgage Default Insurance
- Slug: `/glossary/cmhc-mortgage-default-insurance`
- **Facts to verify:**
  - Required for down payments under 20%
  - Premium rates (0.60% at 20% down, 4.00% at 5% down as of 2024)
  - Max insurable mortgage: $1 million (was $999,999, but 2024 policy raised it to $1.5M effective December 15, 2024 for FTB and newly built homes)
- **Primary source:** https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca

### ☐ 29. Residential Tenancy Rent Increase Caps
- Slug: search "rent increase"
- **Facts to verify:**
  - 2024 cap: 3.5%
  - 2025 cap: 3.0%
  - Annual, one increase per 12-month period
- **Primary source:** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/during-a-tenancy/rent-increases

### ☐ 30. Notice to End Tenancy (Landlord)
- Slug: search "notice to end tenancy"
- **Facts to verify:**
  - Personal use: 4 months notice + 1 month compensation
  - Renovation (renoviction): stricter 2024 rules — landlord must apply for Order of Possession
  - Cause: 10-day or 1-month notice types
- **Primary source:** Residential Tenancy Act + Residential Tenancy Branch guidance
