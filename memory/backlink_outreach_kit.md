## What NOT to do

- **DO NOT** buy backlinks — even one paid link from a link farm can penalize the whole domain. Google's spam team catches these algorithmically now.
- **DO NOT** post the same pitch to 20 outlets copy-paste. Editors talk; you'll get burned.
- **DO NOT** offer money in exchange for coverage. Illegal in Canada under the Competition Act's "hidden advertising" provisions for any journalism outlet.
- **DO NOT** use fake reciprocal networks ("link to my site and I'll link to yours"). Google detects unnatural link patterns; a natural real-estate agent site should have 80%+ inbound / 20% outbound ratio.
