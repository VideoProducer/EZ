## 📐 Designer Notes

- **Trim size:** 8.5 × 11 letter portrait, perfect-bound, 12 pages inside + soft-touch matte cover.
- **Font pairing:** Sora (headlines, 300–700 wt) + Inter or Cotala's house serif (body). Never use Doogie's UI type stack here — this is a print piece.
- **Colour palette:** navy `#0F2A5B`, warm gold `#F5A623`, paper `#FAFAF7`, ink `#111827`. Match the eztofind.ca brand system.
- **Grid:** 12-column with 24 pt margins. Photography full-bleed on cover, spreads 3/6/8/11; text-forward pages (2/5/7/9/10/12) use 8-column body copy with generous 4-column margin.
- **Callouts:** navy `#0F2A5B` background with warm gold headline; body text in cream `#FAFAF7`. Reuse the site's callout-tile motif for cross-medium brand recognition.
- **QR:** cover back / inside back should carry a QR to `eztofind.ca/listing/3015-141-st` so the print piece funnels leads into the digital experience (Matterport cast, priority list, saved-favourite flow).
- **Photo count target:** ~28–32 hero photos across the 12 pages (roughly 3 per spread except cover and floor plan); the remaining ~70 of the 105 Cotala images live on the digital listing page and Matterport.
