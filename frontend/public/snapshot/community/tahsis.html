Tahsis, BC

About Tahsis

Tahsis is a small, remote community located on the northwest coast of Vancouver Island in British Columbia, nestled at the head of Tahsis Inlet, a deep coastal fjord that extends inland from Nootka Sound. It sits approximately 175 kilometres northwest of Campbell River, the nearest regional hub, and is accessible primarily by a lengthy unpaved logging road that winds through rugged mountain terrain, or by water and float plane. The surrounding landscape is defined by steep, forested mountains, the narrow winding inlet, and the lush temperate rainforest characteristic of this part of Vancouver Island. The climate is mild and wet year-round, with moderate temperatures, heavy annual rainfall, and relatively little snowfall at lower elevations, reflecting the region's coastal maritime influence. Tahsis is a remote, rural village with a small population that has declined considerably since its peak during the height of the local forestry industry, today numbering only a few hundred residents. The community has a tight-knit, resilient character shaped by decades of resource-industry culture and a deep connection to the surrounding wilderness. Life here is defined by outdoor pursuits, including fishing, boating, kayaking, and wildlife viewing in some of Vancouver Island's most pristine and least-travelled coastal environments. The area holds significant historical and cultural importance, as Nootka Sound was the site of early contact between European explorers and Indigenous peoples, and the surrounding region remains deeply connected to the heritage of the Mowachaht/Muchalaht First Nation. Residents embrace a self-sufficient, community-minded way of life that reflects the realities and rewards of living in one of British Columbia's more isolated corners. The local economy has historically been anchored in forestry and related industries, which drove the founding and early growth of the community. While industrial activity has diminished over the years, some forestry operations continue in the region. Fishing, both commercial and sport, also plays a meaningful role, and the community has made efforts to develop ecotourism as a source of economic diversification, drawing visitors interested in world-class salmon and halibut fishing, kayaking, and coastal exploration. Basic amenities are available within the community to serve day-to-day needs, though residents often travel to Campbell River for a broader range of goods, services, and healthcare. The pace of life and the accessibility of vast, unspoiled wilderness make Tahsis a distinctive place for those drawn to remote coastal living.
