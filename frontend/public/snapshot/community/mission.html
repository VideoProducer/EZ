About Mission

Mission is a district municipality located in the eastern Fraser Valley, situated along the north bank of the Fraser River in British Columbia. It lies approximately 70 kilometres east of Vancouver and sits directly across the river from Abbotsford, making it well connected to the broader Metro Vancouver and Fraser Valley regional markets. The community is framed by dramatic geography, with the Fraser River defining its southern boundary and the forested slopes of the Coast Mountain range rising to the north. Rollin Lake and a network of trails and green spaces contribute to the area's natural character, while the surrounding lowlands support agricultural land typical of the fertile Fraser Valley. Mission experiences a temperate climate with warm, dry summers and mild, wet winters, benefiting from slightly more precipitation than communities further west due to its position deeper in the valley. Mission is a mid-sized town with a population of roughly 40,000 residents, offering a community that bridges small-town character with growing suburban development. The downtown core retains a historic quality, with the community having deep roots tied to the arrival of the railway and early missionary settlement in the region. The restored Westminster Abbey, a Benedictine monastery perched on a hillside above the town, is one of the area's most recognized and visited landmarks. Residents generally enjoy an outdoor-oriented lifestyle, with ready access to hiking, mountain biking, fishing, and river recreation. The community maintains a strong sense of local identity and civic engagement, drawing families and individuals who appreciate a quieter pace while remaining within commuting range of larger urban centres. Mission's economy draws from a diverse mix of industries including forestry, agriculture, light manufacturing, and retail trade. The town serves as a regional service centre for surrounding rural communities, with a reasonable array of commercial amenities, healthcare services, and educational facilities available locally. Employment is supported by both public sector roles and small-to-medium private enterprises, and the local economy has continued to expand as regional growth in the Fraser Valley draws new residents and businesses eastward. Access to the West Coast Express commuter rail service connecting Mission to Vancouver has made the area increasingly attractive to working professionals who prefer a lower-density lifestyle while maintaining ties to the city. Community amenities support day-to-day needs comfortably, and ongoing development continues to broaden the range of services available to residents. ☀️ Weather & Climate in Mission

Mission, located in the Fraser Valley east of Vancouver, experiences a temperate oceanic climate with influences from the surrounding mountains that can intensify precipitation compared to communities closer to the coast. Winters are mild and persistently wet, with average temperatures typically ranging from around 0°C to 7°C. Snowfall occurs but is generally modest, often melting within days, though Arctic outflow events periodically push cold, dry air westward through the Fraser Canyon, bringing sharper temperature drops and occasionally glazing roads with ice or freezing rain. Rainfall dominates the winter season, and overcast skies are the norm for much of November through February. Summers in Mission are warm and noticeably drier, with average temperatures ranging from approximately 15°C to 27°C and occasional heat events pushing readings higher. The valley's geography channels afternoon breezes that can moderate heat somewhat, but wildfire smoke from interior BC fires occasionally settles into the valley during July and August, reducing air quality. Autumn brings a gradual return of cloud and rain, and dense valley fog is a recurring feature during transitional seasons, a hallmark shared across much of the lower Fraser Valley.
