About Cranbrook

Cranbrook is situated in the southeastern corner of British Columbia, nestled within the Rocky Mountain Trench at an elevation of approximately 940 metres. It serves as the largest city in the East Kootenay region and acts as a regional hub for the surrounding communities. Calgary is roughly 400 kilometres to the east, while Vancouver lies approximately 850 kilometres to the west. The city is framed by the dramatic peaks of the Rocky and Purcell mountain ranges and benefits from the presence of the St. Mary and Kootenay rivers nearby, along with several lakes within the broader region. Cranbrook enjoys a semi-arid continental climate, characterized by four distinct seasons, warm and relatively dry summers, and cold winters with moderate snowfall — conditions well suited to outdoor recreation year-round. Cranbrook has a population of approximately 20,000 residents, making it a small city with a strong regional identity. It functions as an urban centre within a rural and mountainous setting, offering residents a balance of city conveniences and easy access to wilderness. The community has a welcoming, close-knit character shaped by generations of resource-industry workers, ranchers, and more recently, an influx of retirees and remote workers drawn by the lifestyle the region affords. Cranbrook has a rich railway heritage that is reflected in local culture and preserved through notable heritage assets in the community. The downtown core maintains an active presence, and the surrounding area offers opportunities for hiking, skiing, fishing, golfing, and other outdoor pursuits throughout the year. The economy of Cranbrook is anchored by its role as a regional service centre, meaning healthcare, retail, government services, and professional services form a substantial part of the employment base. Forestry and related industries have historically been important to the local economy, and the broader region supports agriculture and ranching in the valley floors. Tourism contributes meaningfully to the local economy, driven by the area's mountain scenery, recreational amenities, and proximity to several provincial parks and ski areas. The Canadian Rockies International Airport, located within the city, provides commercial air service and supports both business travel and tourism. Cranbrook offers a comprehensive range of amenities including post-secondary education, medical facilities, retail shopping, restaurants, arts and cultural programming, and recreational infrastructure, making it a self-sufficient centre for the East Kootenay.
