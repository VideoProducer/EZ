About Wells

Wells is a small community located in the Cariboo region of central British Columbia, nestled in the Cariboo Mountains at an elevation of approximately 1,220 metres. It sits roughly 80 kilometres east of Quesnel, which serves as the nearest regional hub, and approximately 560 kilometres north of Vancouver. The area is characterized by dramatic mountain terrain, dense boreal forest, and a landscape shaped by the gold rush era. Winters in Wells are cold and snowy, with the high elevation contributing to significant snowfall and a lengthy winter season, while summers are cool and pleasant, making the area appealing to outdoor enthusiasts. Wells is a very small, remote community with a population of only a few hundred residents, giving it a distinctly intimate and close-knit character. The town retains much of its historic charm, having originally developed as a gold mining settlement in the 1930s, and many of its heritage buildings and street layouts reflect that era. Adjacent to Wells is Barkerville Historic Town, one of British Columbia's most significant heritage sites, which draws visitors from across the province and beyond. The community has attracted a notable arts presence over the decades, contributing to a creative and culturally engaged atmosphere that is somewhat unique for a settlement of its size and remoteness. Outdoor recreation plays a central role in daily life, with opportunities for skiing, snowshoeing, hiking, and backcountry exploration readily available in the surrounding mountains. The economy of Wells has historically been rooted in gold and hard rock mining, and resource extraction continues to play a role in the regional economic landscape. Heritage tourism connected to the nearby historic townsite is a significant contributor to local economic activity, particularly during the summer months when visitor numbers rise considerably. The arts sector also provides a modest but meaningful economic contribution, supported by cultural programming and residency opportunities that attract artists from outside the region. Given the community's small size and remote setting, everyday amenities are limited locally, with residents typically travelling to Quesnel for a broader range of services, healthcare, and retail. Despite this, Wells maintains basic community services and infrastructure that support year-round living for its residents.
