About Spallumcheen

Spallumcheen is a rural municipality located in the North Okanagan region of British Columbia, surrounding but separate from the city of Armstrong, which sits at its geographic heart. Positioned approximately 50 kilometres north of Kelowna and roughly 430 kilometres east of Vancouver, the municipality occupies a broad, fertile valley framed by rolling hills and the elevated terrain of the Okanagan Highlands. The Shuswap River and Bessette Creek wind through portions of the landscape, supporting both agriculture and natural habitats, while Larkin and the surrounding countryside offer expansive views of open farmland characteristic of the region. Spallumcheen experiences a semi-arid interior climate with warm, dry summers and cold, snowy winters, though its valley setting can also bring distinct seasonal shifts that benefit agricultural production. Spallumcheen is a predominantly rural and agricultural community with a population of approximately 5,000 to 6,000 residents, giving it a quiet, small-community character that stands in contrast to more urbanized centres in the Okanagan. Life here is shaped by the land, with wide open spaces, working farms, and a strong sense of community heritage. The municipality has deep roots in agriculture dating back to early settler and Indigenous history, and that connection to the land continues to define daily life for many residents. The close proximity to Armstrong means that residents have ready access to essential services and community amenities while still enjoying the pace and openness of rural living. The economy of Spallumcheen is anchored largely in agriculture, with the municipality recognized as one of the most productive farming areas in the North Okanagan. Dairy farming, livestock operations, grain production, and mixed farming have historically formed the backbone of the local economy, and active farmland remains a defining feature of the community. The surrounding region also supports employment in light manufacturing, trades, and service industries, with many residents commuting to Armstrong, Vernon, or other nearby centres for work. General retail services, recreational opportunities, and community facilities are accessible within the immediate area and in the neighbouring communities that Spallumcheen wraps around, making the municipality a practical as well as appealing place to live and work.
