About Parksville

Parksville is a small city situated on the east coast of Vancouver Island, approximately 37 kilometres north of Nanaimo and roughly 155 kilometres north of Victoria. The community faces the Strait of Georgia to the east, offering sweeping ocean views and access to some of Vancouver Island's most celebrated sandy beaches, which stretch for kilometres at low tide. The Englishman River flows through the area before emptying into the strait, and the surrounding landscape transitions from coastal lowlands to the foothills of the Vancouver Island Ranges to the west. The region enjoys a mild, marine-influenced climate characterized by warm, dry summers and relatively mild, wet winters, making it one of the more temperate communities on Vancouver Island. Parksville has a population of approximately 13,000 to 14,000 people within the city proper, with the broader regional district encompassing a considerably larger population. The community strikes a balance between a welcoming small-town character and the amenities typically associated with a larger centre. It is well known as a popular destination for both visitors and retirees, drawn by its relaxed pace of life, natural surroundings, and strong sense of community. The nearby Rathtrevor Beach Provincial Park is a notable natural landmark, and the area hosts a variety of seasonal festivals that celebrate its coastal environment. The Oceanside corridor, which includes the neighbouring community of Qualicum Beach, reinforces the region's reputation as a desirable and cohesive coastal community. The economy of Parksville has historically been supported by a combination of tourism, retail trade, and service industries, with tourism playing an especially prominent role given the area's beaches and outdoor recreational opportunities. Forestry has traditionally been part of the broader regional economy, though the local employment base has diversified over time to include construction, healthcare services, and small business activity. The community offers a well-developed range of amenities for its size, including shopping, dining, recreational facilities, and professional services that serve both residents and the steady flow of seasonal visitors. Healthcare and educational services are accessible locally, with more specialized services available in Nanaimo to the south. The Parksville and District area continues to attract new residents and businesses drawn to its combination of natural appeal and practical infrastructure.
