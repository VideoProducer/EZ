About Abbotsford

Abbotsford is located in the eastern Fraser Valley of British Columbia, approximately 70 kilometres east of Vancouver and just north of the Canada-United States border with Washington State. It sits where the fertile flatlands of the Fraser Valley begin to meet the foothills of the Coast Mountains, giving the city a dramatic backdrop of mountain peaks to the north and east. The Fraser River forms part of its northern boundary, while the surrounding lowlands are characterized by some of the most productive agricultural land in the province. Abbotsford experiences a temperate oceanic climate with mild, wet winters and warm, relatively dry summers, making it one of the more agriculturally favourable regions in Canada. Abbotsford is British Columbia's fifth-largest city by population, with approximately 160,000 residents, and carries a character that blends urban convenience with a strong rural and agricultural identity. The city encompasses a mix of established suburban neighbourhoods, rural farmland, and a growing commercial core, giving it a distinctly diverse feel that reflects both its agricultural heritage and its steady growth as a regional centre. Historic downtown districts, cultural spaces, and community events contribute to an active civic life, and residents enjoy access to extensive trail networks, parks, and natural green spaces that take advantage of the surrounding landscape. Abbotsford's economy is anchored by agriculture, which remains central to the region's identity and land use, with the area being one of the leading producers of berries, poultry, and dairy products in BC. Beyond farming, the city has developed a diversified employment base that includes manufacturing, logistics, aerospace, healthcare, and post-secondary education, supported by its position as a transportation hub with access to major highway corridors and a regional airport. Residents benefit from a full range of urban amenities including retail centres, healthcare facilities, recreational venues, restaurants, and entertainment options, while still maintaining proximity to the natural environment. The city's combination of infrastructure, services, and agricultural surroundings continues to attract a wide range of residents and businesses to the region. ☀️ Weather & Climate in Abbotsford

Abbotsford sits within the Fraser Valley and experiences a humid oceanic climate, shaped by its low elevation and proximity to the Coast Mountains. Winters are mild and wet, with average temperatures typically ranging from around 1°C to 7°C between December and February. Snowfall occurs but is generally modest compared to higher elevations, though the region is periodically affected by Arctic outflow events that push cold continental air westward through the Fraser Canyon, causing brief but sharp temperature drops, freezing rain, and icy conditions. Rainfall dominates the winter season, with the valley receiving significant precipitation during these months. Summers in Abbotsford are warm and relatively dry, with average temperatures generally ranging from about 18°C to 26°C between June and August, and occasional heat events pushing values higher. Precipitation drops noticeably in summer, creating a distinct wet-dry seasonal pattern. The valley is susceptible to wildfire smoke during late summer, drifting in from fires across the BC Interior and beyond. Dense radiation fog is a notable autumn and early winter feature, forming overnight in the valley and sometimes persisting through morning hours, a characteristic trademark of Fraser Valley communities.
