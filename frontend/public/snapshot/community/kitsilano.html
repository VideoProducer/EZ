About Kitsilano

Kitsilano is a vibrant waterfront neighbourhood located on the west side of Vancouver, British Columbia, situated along the north shore of the Burrard Peninsula. Bordered by English Bay to the north and west, and nestled between the neighbourhoods of Fairview and Point Grey, Kitsilano enjoys a prime position within one of Canada's most scenic urban settings. The neighbourhood offers sweeping views of the North Shore Mountains, English Bay, and the Strait of Georgia, with Kitsilano Beach serving as one of its most celebrated geographic anchors. The area benefits from Vancouver's mild, temperate climate, characterized by warm, dry summers and relatively mild, wet winters, making outdoor life accessible throughout much of the year. Kitsilano is decidedly urban in character, forming part of the broader City of Vancouver with a population well into the tens of thousands when counted alongside surrounding communities. Known locally as "Kits," the neighbourhood carries a distinctive blend of bohemian heritage and contemporary energy, having evolved from its countercultural roots in the 1960s and 1970s into a polished, family-friendly urban village. Tree-lined residential streets feature a mix of heritage character homes and modern infill development, while the waterfront promenade along Kits Beach remains a beloved community gathering space. The neighbourhood has a strong sense of identity, attracting residents who appreciate walkability, access to green space, and proximity to both downtown Vancouver and quieter residential surroundings. The local economy reflects Kitsilano's urban, educated, and professionally active population. Employment in the neighbourhood spans retail, hospitality, health and wellness, technology, creative industries, and professional services, with many residents also commuting to downtown Vancouver's broader employment base. The commercial corridor along West 4th Avenue and Broadway supports a dense array of independent shops, dining establishments, fitness studios, and service businesses, contributing to a self-sufficient and walkable community environment. The area is well served by transit connections, cycling infrastructure, and community amenities including parks, recreational facilities, and cultural institutions. Healthcare and educational services are accessible within the neighbourhood and across the broader city.
