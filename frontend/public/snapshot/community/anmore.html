About Anmore

Anmore is a small village municipality located in the northeastern corner of the Tri-Cities area within Metro Vancouver, British Columbia. Nestled between Coquitlam and Port Moody, it sits approximately 30 kilometres east of downtown Vancouver, with easy access to the broader Lower Mainland via major regional roadways. The community is defined by its dramatic natural surroundings, positioned at the foot of the Coast Mountains and bordered by Buntzen Lake and the Belcarra Regional Park to the north and east. Buntzen Lake itself is a prominent geographic landmark, offering a large freshwater reservoir set against forested mountain terrain. The climate is characteristic of the Pacific Northwest, with mild, wet winters and warm, relatively dry summers, tempered by the surrounding mountains and the moderating influence of the nearby ocean. Anmore carries the designation of a village, making it one of the smaller incorporated municipalities in Metro Vancouver, with a population of only a few thousand residents. Its character is distinctly rural and semi-rural, standing apart from the denser suburban development found in neighbouring communities. The landscape is dominated by forested lots, generous property sizes, and a quiet, nature-oriented lifestyle that attracts those who value seclusion while still maintaining reasonable proximity to urban amenities. Buntzen Lake Recreation Area serves as a central landmark, drawing both residents and visitors for hiking, swimming, and outdoor recreation year-round. The community maintains a low-density, village atmosphere with a strong sense of connection to the natural environment. Anmore's economy does not have a significant independent commercial or industrial base, as its small population and rural zoning limit the scale of local business activity. Most residents commute to neighbouring municipalities such as Port Moody, Coquitlam, or the broader Metro Vancouver area for employment, professional services, and major retail. The village does support a modest range of local services suited to its scale, and residents benefit from proximity to the fuller range of amenities, healthcare facilities, and commercial centres available in the surrounding Tri-Cities region. Recreation and tourism play a quiet but meaningful role in the local identity, with the area's parks and natural features drawing consistent visitor traffic.
