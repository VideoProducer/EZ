About White Rock

White Rock is a small city located on the southwestern corner of the Semiahmoo Peninsula in the South Surrey area of Metro Vancouver, British Columbia. It sits directly on the Canada-United States border, adjacent to the municipality of Surrey to the north and the Semiahmoo Bay to the south, with the Washington State community of Blaine just across the border. White Rock is approximately 45 kilometres south of downtown Vancouver, making it one of the southernmost communities in the Greater Vancouver region. The city is defined by its position along Semiahmoo Bay, offering sweeping views of the Gulf Islands, the San Juan Islands, and the Olympic Mountains in Washington State. The coastal setting brings a relatively mild, maritime climate, with warmer summers and less snowfall than many other parts of Metro Vancouver, thanks in part to its southerly latitude and proximity to the ocean. White Rock is a compact urban community with a population of approximately 22,000 residents, giving it the feel of a tight-knit seaside town within a larger metropolitan region. The city is well known for its iconic 470-metre pier, one of the longest in Canada, as well as its historic waterfront promenade that stretches along Marine Drive. The large white boulder from which the city takes its name remains a beloved local landmark on the beach. White Rock carries a distinct character shaped by its coastal heritage, attracting residents drawn to a walkable, community-oriented lifestyle with immediate access to the ocean. The mix of heritage homes, beachfront properties, and a vibrant arts and cultural scene contributes to a sense of place that feels somewhat distinct from the broader suburban landscape that surrounds it. The local economy in White Rock is anchored by retail, hospitality, personal services, and tourism, with the waterfront district serving as a hub for dining, boutique shopping, and seasonal visitor activity throughout the year. Healthcare and professional services also represent a meaningful portion of the employment base, supported by regional medical facilities and professional offices serving both White Rock and the broader South Surrey area. The city offers a well-rounded range of everyday amenities, including grocery and specialty retail, dining, recreation facilities, transit connections, and proximity to major commercial corridors in neighbouring Surrey. Its location near a major Canada-US border crossing also creates cross-border economic activity that influences local commerce.
