About Esquimalt

Esquimalt is a township municipality located on the southern tip of Vancouver Island in British Columbia, situated immediately adjacent to the City of Victoria and forming part of the Greater Victoria metropolitan area. The community sits along the Esquimalt Harbour and the Strait of Juan de Fuca, offering a coastal setting defined by sheltered waterways, rocky shorelines, and views across to the Olympic Mountains in Washington State. The region enjoys one of the mildest climates in Canada, characterized by warm, dry summers and mild, relatively wet winters, with minimal snowfall compared to much of the country. Esquimalt is an urban municipality with a population of approximately 17,000 residents, making it a compact but vibrant community that blends seamlessly with greater Victoria while maintaining its own distinct identity. The township has a strong sense of local pride rooted in its long maritime heritage, with a visible naval presence that has shaped its character for well over a century. Public parks, waterfront access points, and recreational amenities contribute to an active outdoor lifestyle, and the area's proximity to downtown Victoria means residents benefit from the full range of urban conveniences while enjoying a slightly more neighbourhood-scaled environment. The economy of Esquimalt is closely tied to its prominent military presence, as the township is home to one of Canada's primary naval bases on the Pacific Coast, making the Department of National Defence one of the most significant employers in the area. Beyond the defence sector, the local economy benefits from its integration into the broader Greater Victoria economy, which encompasses government services, tourism, technology, healthcare, and education. Residents have access to a full range of everyday amenities including grocery and retail options, community recreation facilities, restaurants, and transit connections that link the township to Victoria and the surrounding region. The waterfront and harbour continue to support marine-related industries and services, reinforcing the community's enduring connection to the sea.
