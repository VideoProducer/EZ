About North Vancouver

North Vancouver refers to the two neighbouring municipalities on the north shore of Burrard Inlet — the **City of North Vancouver** (the compact urban core built around Lonsdale Avenue and Lower Lonsdale) and the surrounding **District of North Vancouver** (a much larger mountainous municipality wrapping around the City on three sides, reaching from Deep Cove in the east to the Capilano River in the west and up to Grouse Mountain). Together they form what most residents simply call "North Van" — a north-shore community connected to downtown Vancouver by the Lions Gate Bridge, the Ironworkers Memorial (Second Narrows) Bridge, and the SeaBus passenger ferry that docks at Lonsdale Quay. The area is known for its blend of ocean views, dense old-growth rainforest, quick access to hiking and skiing (Grouse, Seymour, Cypress via West Van), and a distinct North Shore character that feels a world apart from downtown despite being only a 12-minute SeaBus ride away. The **City** side is denser and more urban — a walkable grid of townhomes, condos, and character craftsman homes centred on Lonsdale, with the popular Shipyards District waterfront redevelopment and the Lonsdale Quay Market. The **District** side is more expansive and residential — from the affluent Edgemont Village and Delbrook areas in the west, through Lynn Valley in the middle (a family favourite near Lynn Canyon Park), to Blueridge, Seymour, and the tight-knit seaside community of Deep Cove in the east. Both municipalities operate independent planning departments, zoning bylaws, and property tax rates — but real estate listings, MLS® data, and everyday life blur the boundary. Buyers and sellers typically evaluate North Vancouver as one community, then drill down to specific neighbourhoods for detailed research. Housing spans compact Lonsdale condos, character craftsman homes on the City side, extensive single-family neighbourhoods across the District, and luxury properties along the mountain benches with sweeping views of the harbour and downtown skyline. Because the two municipalities share schools, hospitals, transit, and daily services, most buyers approach the search as "North Vancouver" first — this page unifies both sides so you can browse all active listings and neighbourhoods in one place.
