Sun Peaks, BC

About Sun Peaks

Sun Peaks is a mountain resort community located in the Thompson-Nicola region of British Columbia's Southern Interior, situated approximately 55 kilometres northeast of Kamloops and roughly 450 kilometres northeast of Vancouver. Nestled within the Shuswap Highlands, the community sits at an elevation that shapes its character, surrounded by the peaks of Tod Mountain, Sundance Mountain, and Mount Morrisey. The area's geography is defined by dramatic alpine terrain, forested slopes, and open bowls that transition into the valleys of the surrounding interior plateau. The climate is distinctly continental, bringing cold, snowy winters with reliable snowfall and warm, dry summers, conditions that have made the area well suited to year-round outdoor recreation. Sun Peaks is a small, purpose-built resort community with a population of only a few hundred permanent residents, though it welcomes a significantly larger population of seasonal visitors and part-time residents throughout the year. The community has a compact, pedestrian-friendly village centre with a distinct alpine architectural character, giving it a European mountain village atmosphere that sets it apart from other interior communities. As one of British Columbia's largest ski resorts by skiable terrain, Sun Peaks has developed a strong sense of identity rooted in outdoor recreation and a close-knit community spirit. Despite its relatively remote setting, the community offers a surprisingly cohesive village experience, with a blend of permanent residents and recreational property owners contributing to its unique character. The economy of Sun Peaks is driven overwhelmingly by the resort and tourism industry, with four-season recreation serving as the foundation of local employment and commercial activity. Winter activities including downhill skiing, snowboarding, and cross-country skiing attract visitors from across Canada and internationally, while summer brings hiking, mountain biking, and other outdoor pursuits that sustain tourism activity year-round. Hospitality, accommodation, food and beverage services, and recreation-related retail make up the bulk of the local employment base. The community is supported by a range of amenities within the village, including dining, lodging, and essential services, while residents rely on Kamloops as the nearest regional centre for a broader range of healthcare, retail, and professional services.
