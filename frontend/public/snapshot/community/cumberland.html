About Cumberland

Cumberland is a small village located on Vancouver Island in the Comox Valley region of British Columbia, positioned roughly 10 kilometres southwest of Courtenay and approximately 220 kilometres north of Victoria. The community sits near the base of the Beaufort Range, with the Comox Glacier visible on clear days and the surrounding landscape shaped by forested hills, wetlands, and productive farmland. Comox Lake, a significant freshwater reservoir, lies just to the west and serves as a defining natural feature of the area. The climate is mild and temperate, characteristic of the east coast of Vancouver Island, with warm, dry summers and wet but relatively snow-free winters, making the outdoors accessible and inviting year-round. Cumberland carries a distinct small-town character rooted deeply in its heritage as a historic coal mining town, with its origins dating back to the late 1800s. The village has a tight-knit, welcoming community feel that blends longtime residents with newcomers drawn to the area's natural setting and relaxed pace of life. With a population of just under 4,000, Cumberland maintains a genuine village atmosphere while offering an increasingly vibrant cultural scene shaped by arts, outdoor recreation, and a growing community of young families and outdoor enthusiasts. The historic downtown core preserves a sense of its industrial past, and the surrounding trail network has earned the community recognition as one of British Columbia's premier destinations for mountain biking and outdoor adventure. The local economy has evolved considerably from its coal mining roots and today reflects a broader mix of industries found throughout the Comox Valley. Employment is supported by the regional service sector, construction, tourism, and outdoor recreation industries, with many residents commuting to nearby Courtenay and Comox for work in healthcare, retail, education, and government services. The village itself offers essential everyday amenities including local shops, cafes, and services, while the broader Comox Valley provides access to a full range of commercial, medical, and educational facilities. Cumberland's appeal as an outdoor recreation hub continues to draw visitors and contribute to local economic activity, particularly in the summer months when the trail systems and surrounding wilderness attract guests from across the province and beyond.
