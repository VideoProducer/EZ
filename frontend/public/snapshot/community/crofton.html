About Crofton

Crofton is a small coastal community located on the eastern shore of Vancouver Island in British Columbia, nestled along Osborne Bay where the waters of Stuart Channel separate Vancouver Island from Salt Spring Island. It falls within the Municipality of North Cowichan in the Cowichan Valley Regional District, positioned roughly midway between the cities of Nanaimo to the north and Victoria to the south, with Duncan serving as the nearest regional hub just minutes away. The surrounding landscape is defined by the sheltered waters of the Salish Sea, forested hillsides, and the fertile lowlands of the Cowichan Valley. The area benefits from one of the mildest climates in Canada, characterized by warm, dry summers and relatively mild, wet winters, with significantly less rainfall than much of coastal British Columbia. Crofton is a small, tight-knit village community with a distinctly rural and maritime character. With a population numbering only in the low thousands, it offers a quiet, unhurried pace of life that appeals to those drawn to waterfront living away from the bustle of larger urban centres. The community has deep historical roots, having originally been established in the early twentieth century as a planned townsite connected to copper smelting operations in the region. A ferry terminal connecting Crofton to Salt Spring Island serves as a notable landmark and adds a lively element to the waterfront, making Crofton a natural gateway to the Gulf Islands. The compact downtown area, the working waterfront, and the surrounding green spaces all contribute to the community's distinct small-town identity. Crofton's economy has historically been anchored by industrial activity, most notably a large pulp and paper mill that has been a defining feature of the community for decades and remains a significant local employer. The ferry connection to Salt Spring Island also supports tourism and contributes to local economic activity throughout the year. Beyond its industrial roots, residents benefit from access to a range of everyday amenities including local shops, community services, and recreational facilities both within Crofton and in nearby Duncan, which provides a broader selection of retail, health care, and professional services. Outdoor recreation is a strong draw, with opportunities for boating, fishing, hiking, and exploring the natural coastline readily accessible from the community.
