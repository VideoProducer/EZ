Edgewater, BC

About Edgewater

Edgewater is a small unincorporated community located in the Columbia River Valley in the East Kootenay region of southeastern British Columbia. It sits along Highway 93/95 approximately 17 kilometres north of Invermere, which serves as the nearest regional service hub, and roughly 100 kilometres north of Cranbrook. The community is nestled between the Rocky Mountains to the east and the Purcell Mountains to the west, with the Columbia River and its broad wetlands running through the valley floor. The surrounding landscape is characterized by fertile benchland, forested mountain slopes, and open river flats. Edgewater experiences a semi-continental climate with warm, dry summers and cold winters, receiving notably less precipitation than coastal parts of British Columbia, and enjoying a meaningful number of sunny days throughout the year. Edgewater is a quiet, rural community with a small, close-knit population numbering in the hundreds. Its atmosphere is relaxed and unhurried, reflecting the broader rural character of the Columbia Valley. The community sits within an agricultural landscape that has historically supported ranching and farming, giving it a strong connection to the land and an enduring rural identity. Residents enjoy ready access to outdoor recreation in all seasons, including hiking, cycling, fishing, and wildlife viewing in the surrounding mountains, wetlands, and river corridor. The Columbia River Wetlands, which pass near the community, form one of the longest protected wetlands in North America and represent a significant natural landmark of regional and ecological importance. The local economy is shaped primarily by agriculture, forestry, and tourism, industries that have long defined life in the Columbia Valley. Ranching and hay farming remain visible parts of the economic and physical landscape. Tourism draws visitors to the area year-round, supported by the proximity to hot springs, ski resorts, and provincial parks within the broader valley region. While Edgewater itself offers limited commercial amenities, residents rely on nearby Invermere for a more complete range of services, including retail, dining, medical care, and educational facilities. The community is served by basic local amenities and benefits from its position along a well-travelled highway corridor connecting the region.
