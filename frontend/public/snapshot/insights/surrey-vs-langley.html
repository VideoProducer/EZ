South-of-Fraser Comparison

Surrey vs Langley

BC's largest municipality vs its fastest-growing neighbour

As of 2026-09-19

General information only — not legal, tax, financial, or real-estate advice. Verify with a licensed BC professional before acting.

Surrey and Langley together dominate south-of-Fraser residential inventory. This page compares them at the municipality level — for finer detail see the South Surrey vs White Rock and Fort Langley vs Walnut Grove comparisons.

At a Glance

Surrey
Municipality: City of Surrey
Population: ≈ 620,000 (largest city in BC)
Six town centres (Whalley/City Centre, Guildford, Fleetwood, Newton, Cloverdale, South Surrey). Full housing mix.
Langley
Municipality: Township + City of Langley
Population: ≈ 175,000 combined
Full mix, more detached-heavy than Surrey, growing Willoughby high-rise cluster.
