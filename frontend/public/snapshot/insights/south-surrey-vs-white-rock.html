South Surrey vs White Rock

Two Semiahmoo Peninsula neighbours — how they differ

As of 2026-09-19

General information only — not legal, tax, financial, or real-estate advice. Verify with a licensed BC professional before acting.

South Surrey and White Rock share the Semiahmoo Peninsula and often appear in the same search results, but they are administratively and demographically distinct. This page lays out the factual differences — municipality, housing stock, and market data — so you can decide where to focus your search.

At a Glance

South Surrey
Municipality: City of Surrey
Population: ≈ 78,000 (Surrey census-subdivision share)
Predominantly detached homes on 7,000–14,000 sq ft lots. Subdivisions: Grandview Heights, Morgan Creek, Elgin Chantrell, Ocean Park, Crescent Beach, Sunnyside Park.
White Rock
Municipality: City of White Rock (separate municipality)
Population: ≈ 21,000
Mix of ocean-view condos + walk-up townhomes + 1950s bungalows. Higher condo density than South Surrey.
