Luxury Comparison

Elgin Chantrell vs Morgan Creek

South Surrey's two flagship luxury enclaves

As of 2026-09-19

General information only — not legal, tax, financial, or real-estate advice. Verify with a licensed BC professional before acting.

Elgin Chantrell and Morgan Creek are the two names most quoted when a South Surrey buyer asks about the top of the market. They are not interchangeable — here's how they actually differ.

At a Glance

Elgin Chantrell
Municipality: City of Surrey — south of Crescent Road
Population: ≈ 4,200 households
Detached-only. Half-acre lots typical, some to 1+ acre. Custom builds 4,500–9,000 sq ft. No townhouse or condo.
Morgan Creek
Municipality: City of Surrey — surrounding Morgan Creek Golf Course
Population: ≈ 5,800 households
Predominantly detached (5,500–14,000 sq ft lots), some fee-simple townhouse, gated Morgan Heights enclave to the east.
