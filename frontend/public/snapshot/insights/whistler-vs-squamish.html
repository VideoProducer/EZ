Sea-to-Sky Comparison

Whistler vs Squamish

Resort market vs commuter market — two very different corridors

As of 2026-09-19

General information only — not legal, tax, financial, or real-estate advice. Verify with a licensed BC professional before acting.

Whistler and Squamish are both in the Sea-to-Sky Corridor but function as distinct real-estate markets. Buyer type, financing, and typical price bands differ materially.

At a Glance

Whistler
Municipality: Resort Municipality of Whistler
Population: ≈ 14,000 permanent (60,000+ peak visitor)
Mix of resort strata condos, chalet-style detached, phase-1 vs phase-2 covenant differences (matters for nightly rental).
Squamish
Municipality: District of Squamish
Population: ≈ 24,000
Commuter market — detached, townhouse, low-rise condo. Growing supply of family-oriented new builds (Downtown, Garibaldi Highlands).
