Is now a good time to buy in BC?

How to answer the question without giving advice

As of 2026-09-19

General information only — not legal, tax, financial, or real-estate advice. Verify with a licensed BC professional before acting.

BCFSA rules restrict a licensee from giving financial-advice-style 'now is a good time' statements. What follows is the factual context every 2026 buyer should know before deciding for themselves.

Answers

Bank of Canada rate direction

Interest rate direction affects mortgage carrying cost more than list price does. Check the Bank of Canada announcement schedule and the current fixed vs variable spread with a licensed mortgage broker.

Local MLS® benchmark trend

GVR, FVREB, and RASTS each publish monthly HPI (Home Price Index) benchmarks. Compare the current month vs the same month prior year for a direction signal.

Days on Market (DOM)

Rising DOM = more inventory + slower sale pace. Falling DOM = tightening market. Check the community-level DOM on your target community page.

Sales-to-active-listing ratio

Above 20% = seller's market. Below 12% = buyer's market. 12–20% = balanced. Published monthly by each board.

Your personal financial position

Employment stability, mortgage stress-test result, down-payment saved, closing-cost cushion, and time horizon. Speak with a licensed mortgage broker + accountant.
