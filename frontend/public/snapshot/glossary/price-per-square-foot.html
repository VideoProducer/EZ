Price Per Square Foot

Price per square foot ($/sqft) is the sale price divided by the finished square footage. It's a quick comparison metric but a blunt one — it ignores lot size, view, condition, layout, age, finishing quality, and strata fees. A $750/sqft condo in a leaky building is not comparable to $750/sqft in a 2024 build. Use $/sqft as a sanity check, not a valuation method. A professional Comparative Market Analysis (CMA) is far more reliable.
