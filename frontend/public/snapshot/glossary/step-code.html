Building Code

Step Code

The BC Energy Step Code is a provincial standard, established under the BC Building Code framework, that sets out an incremental series of energy-efficiency performance levels for new construction. Each successive step requires higher energy performance, with the highest steps targeting net-zero-energy-ready construction. Local governments may, under authority provided by the Local Government Act, RSBC 2015, c. 1, reference specific steps within their zoning bylaws or building requirements. Verify current step levels, timelines, and any mandatory provincial baseline requirements with a BC lawyer, notary, or licensed building professional.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the BC Energy Step Code?

The BC Energy Step Code is a provincial standard that provides an incremental approach to achieving higher energy-efficiency requirements for new construction, with the goal of moving toward net-zero-energy-ready buildings. It is administered through the BC Ministry of Energy, Mines and Low Carbon Innovation and the BC Building Code, and local governments may adopt and reference specific Step Code performance levels in their zoning bylaws or development permit area guidelines under the Local Government Act (RSBC 2015, c. 1). The Step Code itself is not mandatory province-wide; its application depends on municipal or regional district adoption. Verify current local requirements with the applicable municipality or a BC lawyer before proceeding with a development project.

Is the BC Energy Step Code mandatory for all new construction in British Columbia?

No. The BC Energy Step Code is a provincial framework that local governments may choose to adopt and require through their bylaws under the Local Government Act (RSBC 2015, c. 1). As of 2026-07-27, implementation varies by municipality: some jurisdictions mandate specific Step Code levels for certain building types, while others have not adopted it at all (verify current). Verify the applicable requirements with the local government where the property is located, or consult a BC lawyer or building professional.

Verify with: BC Energy Step Code
What are the 'steps' in the BC Energy Step Code?

The BC Energy Step Code defines incremental performance targets—Steps 1 through 5 for most building types—that measure energy-efficiency improvements above the base BC Building Code requirements, with Step 5 representing net-zero-energy-ready performance (as of 2026-07-27 — verify current). Each step includes metrics for airtightness, thermal performance, and modelled energy use. Specific technical requirements and metrics are set out in the BC Energy Step Code Building Standard published by the Province; verify the current standard and any local amendments with the applicable municipality or a qualified energy advisor.

Verify with: BC Energy Step Code
Can a local government in BC require compliance with the Step Code for new homes?

Yes. Under the Local Government Act (RSBC 2015, c. 1), municipalities and regional districts have authority to adopt zoning bylaws, development permit area requirements, or building standards that reference specific BC Energy Step Code performance levels for new construction. Many BC municipalities have enacted such requirements as of 2026-07-27 (verify current). Verify the applicable Step Code level, if any, with the local planning or building department for the property in question.

Verify with: BC Energy Step Code
Who verifies Step Code compliance for a new building in BC?

Compliance is typically verified by a qualified energy advisor accredited under a recognized program (such as EnerGuide or equivalent), who conducts energy modelling and on-site testing (for example, blower-door airtightness tests) and issues an Energy Step Code Compliance Report. The local government's building official or planning department reviews the report as part of the building permit process. Verify the specific submission and verification requirements with the municipality where the building is located, and consult a qualified energy advisor or BC building professional.

Verify with: BC Energy Step Code
Does the BC Energy Step Code apply to existing buildings or renovations?

As of 2026-07-27, the BC Energy Step Code framework primarily targets new construction, though some local governments may extend Step Code or similar energy-performance requirements to major renovations or additions through their bylaws (verify current). The provincial standard does not automatically apply to existing buildings unless triggered by local bylaw. Verify with the applicable municipality and consult a BC lawyer or building professional to confirm requirements for renovations or additions to existing structures.

Verify with: BC Energy Step Code
Are there financial incentives or rebates available for meeting Step Code requirements in BC?

Various federal, provincial, and municipal programs have offered rebates or incentives for energy-efficient construction that meets or exceeds Step Code performance levels, though program availability, amounts, and eligibility criteria change over time (verify current as of 2026-07-27). For example, the Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation (CMHC) and BC Hydro have historically offered incentive programs. Verify current programs, amounts, and eligibility with BC Hydro, CMHC, the applicable local government, or a licensed tax professional before relying on any incentive in a financial analysis.

Verify with: BC Energy Step Code
Does compliance with the BC Energy Step Code affect property taxes or the Home Owner Grant?

Meeting BC Energy Step Code performance levels does not directly alter the assessed value methodology used by BC Assessment for property tax purposes, nor does it change eligibility for the Home Owner Grant under the Home Owner Grant Act (as of 2026-07-27 — verify current). However, energy-efficient features may influence market value, which is reflected in the assessment. Verify the impact on your specific property assessment or grant eligibility with BC Assessment or consult a BC lawyer or licensed tax professional.

Verify with: BC Energy Step Code
If I purchase a presale condo in BC that must meet Step Code, what should be disclosed in the disclosure statement?

Under the Real Estate Development Marketing Act (REDMA) and its regulations, a developer's disclosure statement for a presale strata unit must include all material facts, which may include the applicable Step Code level if mandated by local bylaw and material to energy performance or construction cost. The disclosure statement must also describe any unusual or special obligations or restrictions affecting the development. Verify that the disclosure statement addresses applicable Step Code requirements and consult a BC lawyer or notary before purchasing a presale property.

Verify with: BC Energy Step Code
Where can I find official information about the BC Energy Step Code and local adoption?

Official information is published by the BC Ministry of Energy, Mines and Low Carbon Innovation at www.gov.bc.ca, including the BC Energy Step Code Building Standard and implementation guides (verify current as of 2026-07-27). Each local government also publishes its own Step Code requirements, if adopted, on its municipal website or through its planning and building departments. For legal or technical interpretation of how the Step Code applies to a specific property or project, verify with the applicable municipality and consult a BC lawyer, notary, or qualified building professional.
