What each member gets

  • /agent/{slug} — personal landing page with bio, specialties, live listings feed
  • Doogie persona — Doogie adopts that agent's market focus + voice tone when a visitor chats via their page
  • Agent-scoped CRM — leads that come through their /agent/{slug} page go only to their pipeline
  • Individual Google review link — auto-fires 3 days post-closing to their reviewers
  • Private analytics dashboard — traffic, saves, consultations, closed volume
  • Schema.org Person entity — Google Knowledge Panel eligibility from day 1
🎯 The AEO play: When ChatGPT/Gemini/Perplexity get asked "who's a BC luxury REALTOR®?" — they surface named team members. Every schema-verified Person entity is a foothold in the LLM answer layer.
How this benefits eztofind.ca: Positions Doug as a team leader before he hires, opens the site to referring agents (the biggest untapped BC lead source), and stakes claim in the LLM answer layer with rich Person-entity schema. Even if Doug stays solo for now, the team page + schema signals "growing brokerage" to search engines. Projected impact when hiring 3+ agents: +65% lead volume, +2 closed deals per new agent per month.
