Motion + interaction

Every hover is deliberate. Every transition tells the browser you meant it.

Interactions

  • Custom navy-on-gold difference cursor with 20px lag ring
  • Nav underline swipes left→right on hover (280ms cubic-bezier)
  • Buttons arrow-shift 6px on hover, no whole-element scale
  • Focus rings match the primary CTA colour, not blue browser default
  • Every card entrance staggered by 60ms (Motion library)

Materials

  • SVG noise grain overlay at 6% opacity, mix-blend overlay
  • Glassmorphism: 20px backdrop-blur, 4% white fill, gradient edge lines
  • Ambient orbs behind hero — 60px blur, mixed hues, drift 30s
  • Sharp corners (0px radius) on primary surfaces — luxury signal
  • Solid dark backgrounds (never gradients on dark — that muddies)
