EZtoFind.caWeekly Maintenance Checklist
Doug LeMaire, REALTOR®Fraser Property Management Realty Services Ltd. · Target: 2–4 hrs/week
Mon · 30 min

Admin Approval Queue

Approve or reject AI-drafted content awaiting publication

  • Open /admin and log in
  • Review new community synopses — approve or edit
  • Review new weather narratives — approve or reject (fallback: ECCC data)
  • Review glossary FAQs pending review; bulk-approve trusted categories
  • Reject anything referencing specific $ thresholds you can't verify
Weekly · 45 min

Publish One Short Piece

300–500 words on a BC real estate topic. Ask Doogie to draft, then edit.

  • Pick a topic from BCFSA / gov.bc.ca / CREA news feed
  • Write 300 words in your voice; add a personal example
  • Post to LinkedIn (personal profile + company page)
  • Cross-link to relevant glossary term(s) or community page
  • Optional: paste same content into a blog section on the site
Weekly · 30 min

Lead Follow-Up

Every hot lead responded to within 24 hours

  • Open /admin → Leads tab
  • Contact buyer/seller leads by phone or email within 24 hrs
  • Approve or route referral-request leads to network REALTOR®
  • Mark cold or unresponsive leads as "closed"
  • Note referral commissions expected in a simple tracking sheet
Monthly · 60 min

Performance & Regulatory Review

First Monday of the month

  • Google Search Console — top queries + click-through rate
  • Google Business Profile — respond to reviews, post one update
  • Review new REALTOR® applications to referral network
  • Skim BCFSA / CREA / gov.bc.ca updates → note glossary terms to refresh
  • Send updates back to Lovable → re-import to EZtoFind
Quarterly · 2 hrs

Growth Sprint

First Friday of each quarter (Jan · Apr · Jul · Oct)

  • Refresh Featured Listing section (or hide if between listings)
  • Update About page if credentials or focus changed
  • Reach out to 2–3 media contacts with a story hook
  • Send newsletter to subscribers (if list active)
  • Review Environment Canada station mappings for accuracy
  • Rotate any PDFs (BCFSA forms, brokerage docs) to latest revision
  • Audit any changed Doug practice areas in Doogie's system prompt
  • Backup verification — confirm database backups are running
